# AR Vastu – Developers & Real Estate Website

## Overview
A production-ready, ultra-premium real estate business website for **AR Vastu – Developers & Real Estate**, located in Wai, Satara District, Maharashtra, India.
Designed for high trust, luxury architectural aesthetics, bilingual support (English & Marathi), deep local SEO, seamless lead generation (WhatsApp, Call, Site Visit forms), dynamic property catalog with real database integration (Supabase), and an administrative control panel.
